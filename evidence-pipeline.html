The problem

A “source” on a big family-history website is often just another user’s tree.

This process sets a higher bar. Every new person, parent link or date must name an original record (a register entry, a census line, a headstone), quote it, match the person in the tree on at least two details, and survive a check built to disprove it.

Decisions I made

  • AI suggests; code checks what code can check. On the private tree, round 2’s AI helpers averaged 78 back-and-forth steps each. For round 3 I moved the case files, the filter and short review sheets into simple code, and the round ran at about 57k tokens per target.
  • A second AI tries to disprove. The skeptic’s rule is “when you cannot confirm, reject”.
  • A free filter between the two rounds of AI. Before any skeptic time is spent, it drops statements that name no record, match on fewer than two details, repeat a recorded fact, duplicate a child already in the tree, or add a living person.
  • Quotes are matched loosely, not word for word. The filter ignores capital letters, accents, punctuation and field labels, and looks for 80% of the telling words close together. A miss flags the statement for the skeptic instead of dropping it.
  • Nothing is deleted. Disproved parent links are recorded and left out of later counts. The original file is never edited, so every change can be traced to its round and entry.
  • Offline by default. Going online must be switched on, with a fixed pause between requests and a cap on how many.

How it was tested

  • One command runs the public copy start to finish offline, on a made-up family of 420 people.
  • The duplicate scan found all 3 planted duplicates. The merge took 423 people down to 420, and the checker proved none of the 3 merged people lost a fact.
  • The filter kept 12 of the 16 suggested statements and dropped 4: a duplicate child, its parent link, a living person and a fact citing only an online tree.
  • The new family-tree file is rebuilt from the original plus the permanent record, then checked: every original line survives and every link inside the file still works.

Limits

  • Every person, place and record in the public sample and demo is made up; the round numbers come from the private tree.
  • The public code works from saved record pages that the user provides.

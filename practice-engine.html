The problem

Practicing the whole chapter again never targets that one error. Here every question is labeled with the mistake it tests, so practice goes straight at it.

Decisions I made

  • Practice the mistake, not the topic. Each question belongs to exactly one mistake.
  • Speed is part of mastery. A slow right answer doesn’t count toward the streak.
  • Mastery needs different questions. One question right over and over only proves memory.
  • A missed question is retired. After its explanation is shown, a later right answer could just be memory.
  • Pacing gets its own grade. Time on right answers is reported apart from time lost on wrong ones.

How it was tested

  • Every part tried: practice, timed sets, progress, pacing and the quality check.
  • Only checked questions are shown. All 21 sample questions are checked by a program.
  • Bad question files are refused. A quality check flags wrong answer keys, repeated choices and near-copies, and one failing row refuses the whole file.
  • The rules were tested in real study.

Scope

  • One user, on your own computer, with no sign-in.
  • The public code is a general version of a larger study tool I built for myself.
